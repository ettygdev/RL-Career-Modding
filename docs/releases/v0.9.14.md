# RLCareer Overhaul v0.9.14

This release covers the changes since **RL Career Modding v0.3.12**, the previous published release. The project was also developed locally as Career+ and is now called **RLCareer Overhaul**.

## What changed since v0.3.12

- **Single Windows executable.** The new launcher carries the BakkesMod integration, RLBot mechanics and Quick Chat hooks. It can run the Hub alone or detect FilstaNet Modifier in the same folder. The new icon and the **Overhaul v0.9.14** badge identify it in Windows and the Hub.
- **New focus.** The former Y feed, player DM and social relationship overhaul remains in the historical builds. This branch focuses on club management, cosmetics, presentation and gameplay. Coach profiles integrate with the Hub’s social world.
- **Garage and inventory.** Added a club-funded cosmetics shop and owned-items view, painted variants, per-preset color controls and selectable titles. Presets are applied through BakkesMod.
- **Match presentation.** Added loading, spectator and played-match HUDs, team crests, event and player tracking, overtime timing, real-player camera presets where available, and post-game statistics. Intermissions load the next arena in the background and have music and a fallback scoreboard if capture fails.
- **Tactics and communication.** Added configurable D-pad Quick Chat, bot calls and reactions, and separate team-tactics controls. Kickoffs assign one taker with supporting roles.
- **Player development.** Added eight player attributes, Composure, pressure and fatigue. Existing careers are populated on load. The World Editor supports the new player attributes and custom coaches.
- **Coaches and scouting.** Added coach profiles and contracts, club staff and mental training, scout recruitment and management, assignments, hidden external ratings and reports with varying accuracy.
- **Club investment.** Added separate per-player equipment purchases, facility Wi-Fi subscriptions and online technical incidents. Opponent clubs can also make equipment and facility choices.
- **Series administration.** Added tactical timeouts, coach calls with confirmation, opponent timeouts and a dedicated timeout display. The virtual referee can manage technical pauses, neutral kickoffs and game restarts as applicable.
- **Bot gameplay.** Added broader aerial and ground mechanics, including controlled first touches, resets and reset pops, flicks, pinches, ceiling plays and double taps. Bots have more options for switching plans mid-move, recovery on different surfaces, aerial and ground passing, and rotation coverage. Ratings change execution precision.
- **Windows setup.** Added optional Steam Input support for the Epic build, version notifications and Modifier compatibility messaging.

## Download

**RLCareer-Overhaul.exe** is the only attached download. Place it beside **RL Career Hub.exe**. The source is tracked in the repository under [Overhaul](../../Overhaul/), and this tag is the release source snapshot.

Tested locally with Hub **0.2.0-beta.33** and Modifier **0.11.4** as the compatibility target. Bot completion rates remain a tuning area. See the [full guide](../../README.md) for installation and usage.
